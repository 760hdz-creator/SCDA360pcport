## v0.1.21

### Fixed
- **Flickering while climbing the rope in the Sea of Okhotsk storm (#32).** Sam, the rope, the winch and nearby
  crates vanished every other frame. The game checks every frame whether objects are hidden behind others. The port
  could answer one of those checks twice, and the second answer wiped out the game's next check. The game then read
  "hidden" and skipped the objects. Each check now gets exactly one answer. The same lost checks also happened on
  Cozumel 2, Kinshasa 1 and New York 2. They're gone on all levels.
- **Flickering crane and scaffolding in Shanghai (#41).** Lights on the crane and the scaffolding below dropped out
  in triangles while the camera moved. A lighting pass and the surface it lights land at exactly the same depth.
  The tiny offset the game adds to separate them was lost on the PC GPU. The port now adds that offset itself.
- **Mouse wheel walking speed (#35).** As in the PC version, the wheel now sets Sam's walking speed (3 speeds plus
  slow sneak). It only applies while Sam is walking, so it no longer clashes with other actions.
- **Mine assembly with the mouse wheel (#39).** In the JBA HQ mine assembly, the wheel now sets the arm speed:
  reverse, stop and 3 speeds forward, as in the PC version.

### Notes
- The water reflections and the underwater lights in the Sea of Okhotsk (#32), and the lighting at the start of
  Okhotsk (#36), are still open.
- Keypads (#38): still investigating. A save just before a keypad would help.
- For testing: `SCDA_QUERY_MULTIWRITE=1` turns the #32 fix off.
- If you're on v0.1.5 or older, run the new `SCDA_Setup.exe` once in update mode. After that, updates install from
  F1 > Update.
- The installer is unsigned: SmartScreen > More info > Run anyway.
