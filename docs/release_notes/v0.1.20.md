## v0.1.20

### Fixed
- **Sky wedges at the Cozumel horizon (#28).** Bright, flickering wedges of sky could show between the sea and the
  horizon on the cruise ship, and they changed every frame even with the camera still. The far sea is one huge
  polygon around the ship. Where its edges passed behind the camera, the PC GPU computed its depth slightly wrong,
  and the sky drawn behind it showed through. The recomp now cuts those edges itself before the GPU draws them.
  Checked on the curtain save from #28 and on a balcony save.

### Notes
- The fix runs only on the Cozumel sea, so other levels and performance are unchanged.
- For testing: `SCDA_NO_CLIP_GS=1` turns the fix off.
- The water hole in the Sea of Okhotsk (#32) and the lighting at the start of Okhotsk (#36) are still open.
- If you're on v0.1.5 or older, run the new `SCDA_Setup.exe` once in update mode. After that, updates install from
  F1 > Update.
- The installer is unsigned: SmartScreen > More info > Run anyway.
