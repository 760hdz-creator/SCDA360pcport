## v0.1.11

### New
- **Achievements (#6).** The game still reports its Xbox 360 achievement unlocks, and the recomp now catches them.
  Each unlock shows a popup at the top of the screen with the achievement's name, gamerscore and picture, all read
  from your game disc.
  - Unlocks are saved on your PC in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SCDARecomp\profile\achievements.txt`. Nothing is sent to Xbox
    Live.
  - F1 / L3+R3 > **Achievements** lists them, unlocked ones first.
  - 21 achievements (600 G) can be earned in single player. The other 17 (ranks, trophies, map experts) are
    multiplayer-only and are shown greyed out, since the recomp has no multiplayer.
  - Secret achievements stay hidden until you unlock them.
  - Achievements earned before this version were not recorded. Earn them again (for example, finish the mission
    again) and they're added.

### Notes
- Names follow the in-game language setting (English, French, German, Italian, Spanish).
- If you're on v0.1.5 or older, run the new `SCDA_Setup.exe` once in update mode. After that, updates install from
  F1 > Update.
- The installer is unsigned: SmartScreen > More info > Run anyway.
